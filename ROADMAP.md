# Little Pencil Club roadmap

Little Pencil Club is a paper-first drawing game for **ages 5–10**. Build for a child to start on their own, learn a move they can reuse, and end with a drawing that feels like theirs. The order below is a priority list, not a release promise.

## Already shipped

- [x] Paper-first Back, Replay, and Next; optional in-lesson trace and blank canvas.
- [x] 32 original lessons across six topics, with all three numeric difficulty levels represented.
- [x] Local Sketchbook, digital PNG export, and post-lesson remix prompts.
- [x] Printable Pencil Pal cards.

## Unified ranked backlog

**Partial** means a relevant foundation exists; **missing** means the proposed capability was not found in the current repo; **verify** means current coverage needs a focused check. Checked means shipped, not merely scoped. The first five are the best candidates to break into tasks; lower ranks are ideas, not commitments.

1. [ ] **[Partial] Teach one reusable skill in Little fox.** Show light and dark marks side by side, pause for a scrap-paper try, then use the marks on the fox’s tail. Let the child choose fluffy, striped, or dragon-scorched, and invite them to use the same trick on a different subject without a trace. **Done when:** the entire loop works on paper with Replay, Next, and Skip, and the child can point to the choice they made. [Issue #1](https://github.com/Shane-Neeley/little-pencil-club/issues/1)
2. [ ] **[Partial] Make Easy / Medium / Hard clear and self-selected.** Keep the existing three pencil marks and numeric difficulty data; make all three names findable and consistent (the hardest is currently labeled Challenge). Define **Easy** as one big, clearly demonstrated action at a time; **Medium** as combining two moves with a creative choice; **Hard** as an optional independent challenge with less scaffolding. **Done when:** the library signals difficulty in text as well as with icons, and a child can pick or skip a challenge without an age gate, sign-in, or locked core lesson. [Issue #2](https://github.com/Shane-Neeley/little-pencil-club/issues/2)
3. [ ] **[Missing] Test the fox lesson before expanding the library.** Try the current replay and a skill-first paper prototype with willing learners across the age range, with caregiver permission. Observe independent start, cues needed, and whether the child uses the skill on an unmodeled subject. **Done when:** anonymous observations support a documented iterate-or-expand decision. Expand only if most learners in the small pilot can reuse the skill unprompted and younger learners can start on their own. Do not collect names, photos, or recordings. [Issue #3](https://github.com/Shane-Neeley/little-pencil-club/issues/3)
4. [ ] **[Partial] Link lessons by skill, not just subject.** Start with light/dark and looking closely at shapes or textures. For the latter, try an everyday object or leaf: look, draw one part, look again. **Done when:** at least two skills each have a short demo, a guided subject, and another subject that uses the same skill with less help.
5. [ ] **[Partial] Try one optional Sketchbook reflection.** Ask a child to point to or say what they changed, with a skip and no score. **Done when:** the prompt works for paper drawers and does not require typing, an upload, or a network account. Defer studio decoration, currencies, streaks, and automated critiques until there’s a clear reason to build them.

6. [ ] **[Partial] Make the first drawing obvious.** Keep the existing no-account immediate play; test whether the paper and screen choices need a clearer cue before adding another screen. Done when a new learner reaches the first mark without an account or intake form.
7. [ ] **[Partial] Make lessons usable with little reading.** Add clear action icons, one action per prompt, and optional read-aloud or original audio if testing shows value. Done when an early reader can follow a lesson and mute or replay support without leaving it.
8. [ ] **[Partial] Add original reference views.** Show an actual object or rights-cleared reference next to, not over, the child’s drawing; contrast it with the simplified example. Done when a child can hide the model for independent practice and switch it back on.
9. [ ] **[Partial] Add more ways to learn at your own pace.** Preserve Back/Replay/Next, existing resume and reduced-motion handling; consider adjustable speed or a still-step option. Done when pausing, going back, or reloading does not lose the learner’s place or work.
10. [ ] **[Partial] Make a free-draw Playground.** Surface independent drawing and remix prompts without requiring lesson completion. Done when children can start blank, grab or skip an idea, and keep their own drawing. Keep existing trace and paper modes genuinely optional.
11. [ ] **[Missing] Offer a “what should I draw next?” suggestion.** Use a locally chosen topic, level, or practiced skill; keep all paths open and switchable. Done when recommendations can be changed or reset with no profile, date of birth, or tracking.
12. [ ] **[Partial] Add a repeat-and-revisit path.** Revisit a familiar skill in a later session with a new subject instead of replaying the identical answer. Done when at least one skill can be retried and revisited without a streak, deadline, or push notification.
13. [ ] **[Verify] Do an accessibility and input pass.** Cover large touch targets, non-color level indicators, focus and keyboard navigation, contrast, reduced motion, and pen/finger/mouse options. Done when the first lesson is tested on touch and keyboard and supports Apple Pencil without making it a requirement.
14. [ ] **[Partial] Make portable paper activities.** Build on the printable Pencil Pal cards with a printable skill mini-lesson or reference sheet. Done when it can be printed in black and white and finished with ordinary pencil and paper, without a connection.
15. [ ] **[Partial] Set up an original lesson authoring checklist.** Build on the existing lesson data. Done when a new lesson is checked for valid paths/steps, alternate prompts, level, estimated time, reference rights, and a usable transfer exercise; changes to a demo update related modes consistently.
16. [ ] **[Partial] Make progress and saves resilient.** Keep local Sketchbook and PNG export; consider local restore/export and offline play as a web app. Done when offline restart, partial saves, storage limits, and clearing site data have tested, understandable outcomes.
17. [ ] **[Verify] Add a focused cross-device QA checklist.** Verify Safari/iPad with pen and finger, Android touch, and desktop; test resume, redraw, export and Back. Done when a repeatable release check exists; do not promise device support without evidence.
18. [ ] **[Missing] Try a local paper-art Journal.** Consider a caregiver-initiated photo import only after paper creators ask for it. Done when choosing one image is optional, local-only, explicitly removable, and never uploads; the Journal is usable without it.
19. [ ] **[Missing] Try optional short teacher-style demos.** Produce one original hand-and-pencil clip with a visual example, captions and a pause, then compare it with the silent replay. Done when it earns its additional weight by making a skill easier to start or understand.
20. [ ] **[Partial] Build private progress around what was tried.** Use the existing local progress to highlight skills explored, favorites or before/after work. Done when kids can celebrate a decision or practice attempt without a score, leaderboard, or locked essentials.
21. [ ] **[Missing] Add a Pencil Pal story or seasonal studio prompt.** Prototype one short, original seasonal drawing quest or character story inspired by the idea of themed extras, not a copy. Done when it can be played independently and does not require a date, streak or personal info.
22. [ ] **[Missing] Try optional studio decoration and artwork animations.** Start with one locally earned or freely selected room decoration or pencil animation. Done when it’s optional, respects reduced motion, and does not delay or gate drawing.
23. [ ] **[Missing] Explore optional Sparks-like progression.** If basic progress is not enough, try optional cosmetic rewards for variety or practice, not attendance or accuracy. Done when a comparison shows it helps children return to drawing without frustration, pressure, or gating core lessons.
24. [ ] **[Partial] Explore more original paths and skill range.** Put cartoon expressions, environments, clothing, short comics, perspective, layering and composition behind demand and the learning-pilot results. Done per path when there is clear scaffolding, independent practice, and original art.
25. [ ] **[Missing] Explore localization.** Make the first lesson and navigation available in a second language, with independently selectable narration if added. Done when language changes do not reset progress and copy/captions have been checked by a fluent reader.
26. [ ] **[Missing] Explore separate local sketchbooks for siblings.** Keep names optional and do not collect ages. Done when a caregiver can move between and remove local sketchbooks without mixing work. Cloud accounts or cross-device sync are a separate, deferred decision.
27. [ ] **[Missing] Explore a caregiver or classroom guide.** Give adults simple non-grading prompts and short printable activities, not a student monitoring dashboard. Done when one printable session works without child accounts or sending drawings anywhere.
28. [ ] **[Missing / defer] Consider native apps and adult-only subscriptions.** Web first. Revisit app stores, account recovery, restore purchases, and pricing only after demand is clear. Done if pursued when play and payment are separate, guardian controls are explicit, and the paid rules are tested. No near-term monetization decision.
29. [ ] **[Missing / defer] Consider optional human encouragement before automated critique.** Begin with a local caregiver prompt. Any off-device teacher workflow would require a separate child-privacy, consent, deletion and moderation design before development. No default uploads, open chat or public gallery.
30. [ ] **[Not planned] Automated feedback on artwork.** Simply’s privacy notice identifies Tom as AI-powered for voluntarily shared drawings, and describes video responses and voice suggestions; wider feature availability and immediacy are unverified. We will not score children’s artwork or upload it for machine feedback under this roadmap.
31. [ ] **[Missing] Try a same-room Draw Together activity.** Take turns advancing one shared paper-first prompt while each child draws separately. Done when solo play still works and two children can keep separate art without cloud accounts or sharing.
32. [ ] **[Missing / defer] Consider cross-device progress and Journal backup.** Test local export/restore first; require a separate minimization and deletion design before any cloud sync. No child account required for core play.
33. [ ] **[Missing / defer] Explore an on-device drawing replay export.** If drawing-process export proves useful, use locally saved strokes and an explicit export action, not camera or wearable capture. No hosted gallery, recipient comments, or automated capture planned.

## Evidence and scope

- Simply Draw documents [onboarding and the starter fish](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10258428-how-soon-will-i-get-to-draw-something), [paths, skill lessons, Playground and photo Journal](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/10260104-what-is-simply-draw), [paper-only drawing](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256539-simply-draw-most-common-faqs), [Playground levels and Sparks](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15859831-playground-levels-sparks), [Studio](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/14636876-what-is-my-studio), [Tom’s Stories](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15055217-what-is-tom-s-stories), [optional teacher messages](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/15061680-send-my-drawings-to-the-teacher), [language controls](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/12150988-changing-the-app-language-in-simply-draw), [family profiles](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/12151347-add-delete-or-reset-a-profile), and [subscriptions](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/9256419-subscribe-to-simply-draw). Additional documented experiences include [Draw Together](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/16297111-draw-together-ios-only), [multi-device login](https://draw-help.hellosimply.com/en/articles/11111942-log-in-on-multiple-devices), its [privacy description of Tom and sharing](https://www.hellosimply.com/legal/privacy), and a [wearable capture integration](https://www.hellosimply.com/it/ai-glasses-from-meta). Reference photos and particular video-pause behavior are not confirmed across versions. This is a public-documentation inventory, not an exhaustive hands-on audit; platform, language and account eligibility vary. An existing difference worth preserving: Little Pencil Club already supports paper, trace, and blank digital drawing, with browser-local saves and no sign-in.
- [Vlach & Carver: observation coaching in kindergarten](https://ecrp.illinois.edu/v10n1/vlach.html). In one class of 22, explicit prompts to check a model before, during, and after drawing improved observation and the detail of drawings. This supports trying a concrete look-again cue; it does not establish app outcomes or make realism the goal.
- [Bremner et al.: cube drawing among 7–10-year-olds](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10953929/). In one experiment, tracing did not improve subsequent independent cube drawing; highlighting faces rather than edges helped. That narrow task supports optional tracing and an untraced check, not a blanket claim against tracing.
- [California Visual Arts standards](https://www2.cde.ca.gov/cacs/arts?c0=5&c3=3,9,5). Early grades include describing the process and explaining choices. This informs optional show-and-tell, not a quiz or an art grade. Difficulty routes and transfer tests here are product hypotheses, not educational benchmarks.
