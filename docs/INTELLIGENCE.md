# Optional drawing intelligence

**Plan only.** Little Pencil Club needs no AI key for lessons, private-class infrastructure, or local motion effects. Provider features are off by default and are not part of classroom rollout. Use synthetic or adult-owned test images only until the school/caregiver, privacy, and provider gates below are met.

## Keep photos on the device first

1. Let the learner or grown-up preview and crop to the paper before submission. Decode to bounded raster pixels and re-encode without metadata. Avoid filenames, names, account IDs, classroom identifiers, logs containing images, and face recognition or identity matching.
2. Test on-device signals for a face/person, photographic content, or likely identifying text. If a signal is detected, the result is uncertain, or screening is unavailable, do not persist or share the image; show a friendly retake prompt or let an adult help crop it locally. Do not upload the unscreened original for a second opinion. Local checks can miss details; do not claim they prove a drawing was made by a child.
3. Only a locally cleared image may enter a future authorized review flow. Keep peer visibility off until an authorized adult explicitly approves the drawing. A QR invite does not count as consent. Rejected or uncertain images never enter the gallery or generation, and uploads do not fall back to an unapproved service.

## Two independent optional provider capabilities

| Capability | Proposed first-party option | No-AI baseline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Second-pass screening, not sole approval | The [OpenAI Decisions API](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions) accepts image inputs with `gpt-6-luna`. Ask narrowly about drawing likelihood, a real person or photo, and likely identifying text; return allow, retake, or local adult review with a short non-identifying reason code. Use only already-cropped, locally cleared inputs if separately approved. Never include identified text or produce an identity guess. Ambiguity, failure or timeout must not open the cloud upload. | Local checks plus caregiver/teacher review under the applicable pilot rules. If safe screening or review is unavailable, keep the photo submission option off. Built-in digital canvas exports are a simpler pilot input. |
| Make a drawing come to life | Compare a supported [Image API edit or animated GIF](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation) against the local version, on approved art only. The docs describe animated GIF for supported models; confirm model/format, retention and account access together before choosing one. Preserve the original and recognize the child’s lines. | A local bounce, wiggle, or simple cutout animation with replay, pause, and reduced-motion support. Core lessons still work when generation is off. |

A fork would opt into a **server-side** screening and/or animation adapter; neither adapter nor portability is implemented by this plan. Configure the deployment owner's own provider key in server-side secret storage, never browser settings, public config, Git, or a printed code. Separate the adapters and feature switches; do not send art to a different provider automatically. Use a small per-class budget and size/concurrency caps. Any generated output remains private, requires its own adult check before sharing, and is deleted with the source. If the provider is off, animation falls back to local effects, not to an external service.

## Before enabling anything for real classrooms

- Compare with synthetic/adult-owned fixtures, including drawings of faces versus photographed faces, names or school logos in the crop, hands/backgrounds, dim and blurry photos, mixed media, invalid files, and attempts to steer the classifier using text inside the image. Track risky false accepts separately from ordinary retakes; a missed high-risk fixture blocks rollout. Check recognizable strokes, distracting changes, cost, latency, and graceful failure. A small clean fixture run is not proof of real-world safety.
- Confirm an appropriate school and caregiver authorization path, approved processors and purpose, access, short retention and deletion, including generated assets. The [FTC’s school guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions) limits when a school can authorize collection on parents’ behalf. This is an operational gate, not a legal determination or a claim that a classifier creates compliance.
- [OpenAI’s under-18 guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks/under-18-api-guidance) calls for Zero Data Retention before processing personal data of children under 13 or the applicable digital-consent age. [OpenAI’s data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) specify account and endpoint/model eligibility and an exception for images flagged as potential CSAM. Do not assume an API key gives the required configuration, that GIF-capable models are eligible, or that no image could be retained. Do not enable cloud processing for this pilot without a verified policy fit and explicit approval.
